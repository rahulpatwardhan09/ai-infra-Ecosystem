The strategy

The bet

That the APAC sovereignty wave — the Taiwan–Japan–US triangle — is a bigger prize than the US spot market, and that being the national partner beats being the cheapest cloud.

Deal-making

Natural counterparty

APAC-rooted inference platforms and AI startups needing regional serving capacity, plus governments and manufacturers building sovereign capability.

Buyer fit

Choose GMI Cloud when

You need APAC capacity, data residency in Asia, or Blackwell-class hardware without hyperscaler pricing.

Look elsewhere when

Your workload is US or EU latency-bound.
