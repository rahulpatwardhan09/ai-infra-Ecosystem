The two-sided view

What FriendliAI sells — and what it buys

Atomic unit sold

Tokens and inference infrastructure in three shapes: serverless per-token endpoints over open models, Dedicated Endpoints for custom and fine-tuned models, and Friendli Container — the same engine running inside a customer’s own GPU cluster. 540,000+ Hugging Face models deploy one-click; 99.99% uptime SLA on geo-distributed infrastructure.

What they buy

GPU capacity across regions and clouds — asset-light by design. Fleets of GPUs behind the SLA are sourced, not owned; the 2026 Samsung Cloud Platform alliance for NVIDIA B300 inference capacity is the visible tip. This is the clearest demand-side posture of any card on this ladder: FriendliAI is a perpetual GPU buyer.

The strategy

The bet

That inference is a software problem, not a real-estate problem. Founder Byung-Gon Chun invented continuous batching (published as Orca at OSDI 2022, now industry-standard in LLM serving), and the company’s wager is that custom kernels, caching and speculative decoding squeeze enough extra tokens out of every GPU that Friendli can stay asset-light and rent capacity while competitors buy data centers. Same rung as DeepInfra, opposite posture — two ways to sell a token.

Deal-making

Natural counterparty

On the sell side: enterprises with custom or fine-tuned models — the LG EXAONE pattern — and APAC-rooted AI companies needing regional serving. On the buy side: capacity providers. Because Friendli procures GPUs across regions and price points continuously, it is a natural demand-side counterparty for every supply-side company on this ladder — the APAC overlap with GMI Cloud is the most obvious pairing.

Buyer fit

Choose FriendliAI when

You serve a custom or fine-tuned model and want frontier-grade latency and cost without owning infrastructure — or you want the inference engine deployed inside your own GPU cluster.

Look elsewhere when

You just need cheap catalog-model tokens at massive scale and don’t care whose engine serves them.
