Reading this diagram: vCluster Labs and NVIDIA DSX OS occupy the same orchestration layer — they are complementary, not competing. DSX OS provides GPU scheduling, health monitoring, and provisioning primitives. vCluster adds the tenant isolation layer (one K8s API per team), vNode adds kernel-native workload sandboxing, and vMetal handles bare metal lifecycle. Together they form the complete multi-tenant AI factory operating stack. NCP = NVIDIA Cloud Partner.
